# Governance

hamonn is maintained by its founding maintainer(s) listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`. Decisions are made in public, in issues
and ADRs (`docs/adr/`). Anyone can propose a change by opening an issue or PR; maintainers merge when CI is green and the
change fits the spec. Changes to the kernel's rules, the port interfaces, or the coverage gates need an ADR. New maintainers
are invited after sustained, high-quality contributions. The project is MIT licensed and will stay that way.
